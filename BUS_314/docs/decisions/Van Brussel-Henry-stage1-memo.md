Executive Summary
This memo initiates a comprehensive accounting and performance ratio analysis of Caterpillar Inc. (NYSE: CAT), a global leader in heavy machinery and equipment, for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024. Using audited financial statement data sourced from Caterpillar's FY2024 10-K filing with the SEC, the analysis will compute and interpret ratios across five categories: performance, profitability, efficiency, leverage, and liquidity. Key inputs include net sales of $64.8B, net income of $11.6B, total assets of approximately $87B, and a market capitalization derived from a $349.58 share price with 492M shares outstanding. The objective is to assess CAT's financial health, operational efficiency, and value creation in FY2024, yielding actionable insights for investor and stakeholder audiences.

Company Background & Objectives
Company: Caterpillar Inc. (NYSE: CAT) Industry: Industrials — Heavy Machinery & Equipment Fiscal Year Under Review: FY2024 (January 1 – December 31, 2024) Prior Year (Comparison): FY2023 (January 1 – December 31, 2023) Data Source: Caterpillar 10-K FY2024, SEC EDGAR
Caterpillar Inc. is the world's leading manufacturer of construction and mining equipment, diesel and natural gas engines, industrial gas turbines, and diesel-electric locomotives. Operating across more than 190 countries, CAT is widely regarded as a bellwether for global industrial activity and capital expenditure cycles.
A rigorous ratio analysis is valuable for two reasons. First, it translates raw financial statement figures into standardized, comparable metrics that reveal trends in profitability, efficiency, and financial risk. Second, it enables benchmarking against prior-year performance and industry peers, supporting informed capital allocation decisions.
The results of this analysis will inform a stakeholder-facing interpretation (Stage 4) and may be used to evaluate whether CAT is creating economic value above its cost of capital, how effectively it deploys its asset base, and whether its capital structure poses meaningful financial risk.

Approach
Data Sources: All figures are drawn from Caterpillar's audited FY2024 10-K filing, as provided in the course scenario workbook (BUS314_CAT_Scenario.xlsx). The workbook contains three primary financial statements — Balance Sheet, Income Statement, and Cash Flow Statement — plus a pre-structured Ratios tab with named ranges and formula references.
Ratio Categories: The analysis covers five ratio families:
Performance — Market Value Added (MVA), Market-to-Book ratio, and Economic Value Added (EVA) to assess value creation relative to invested capital.
Profitability — Return on Assets (ROA), Return on Capital (ROC), and Return on Equity (ROE), calculated on both start-of-year and average-balance bases.
Efficiency — Asset turnover, receivables turnover, average collection period, inventory turnover, days in inventory, profit margin, and operating profit margin.
Leverage — Long-term debt ratio, debt-equity ratio, total debt ratio, times interest earned, cash coverage ratio, debt burden, and leverage ratio.
Liquidity — Net working capital to assets, current ratio, quick ratio, and cash ratio.
Methodology: All ratio outputs will be calculated using Excel named-range formulas as specified in the Ratios tab. Key analyst assumptions include a cost of capital (WACC) of 7.9%, an effective tax rate of 21.4%, a share price of $349.58, and 492M shares outstanding. Where averages are required (e.g., average equity, average total assets), beginning- and end-of-year balance sheet figures will be averaged. The Du Pont decomposition will be used to cross-check ROA and ROE via their component ratios (leverage, asset turnover, operating profit margin, and debt burden).
Results will be structured to allow non-technical stakeholders to follow the logic from inputs through to interpretive conclusions without requiring accounting expertise.

Limitations & Next Steps
Known Limitations:
Single-year scope: The analysis covers FY2024 and FY2023 (prior year for balance sheet averaging). Multi-year trend analysis would provide a more robust picture of trajectory.
No peer benchmarking: Without direct competitor data (e.g., Deere & Co., CNH Industrial), absolute ratio values are harder to contextualize. Industry averages would strengthen conclusions.
WACC assumption: The 7.9% cost of capital is an analyst estimate. Sensitivity analysis around this assumption would be advisable before presenting EVA conclusions to investors.
Off-balance-sheet items: Operating leases and pension obligations, if material, may understate leverage; this analysis does not adjust for them.
Market data timing: The $349.58 share price reflects the fiscal year-end closing price, which may not reflect current market conditions.
